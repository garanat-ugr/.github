# GARANAT Research Group

**Astrophysics · Stellar Physics · Asteroseismology · Open Science**

GARANAT is an astrophysics research group based at the **University of Granada (UGR), Spain**.

Our research focuses on understanding stellar structure, evolution, and variability through observations, theoretical modelling, and computational methods.

## Research

Our scientific activities include:

- Stellar physics and evolution.
- Asteroseismology and stellar pulsations.
- Analysis of photometric and spectroscopic observations.
- Computational methods and statistical analysis of astronomical data.
- Participation in international astrophysics collaborations and space missions.

## Open Science & Reproducibility

GARANAT promotes transparent, reproducible, and reusable scientific research.

We aim to provide dedicated GitHub repositories accompanying our scientific publications, including, where appropriate:

- Scripts for reproducing published figures and results.
- Curated datasets and links to external scientific archives.
- Documentation of methods, software, and computational environments.
- Versioned research outputs with persistent identifiers.

Our goal is to facilitate independent verification, scientific collaboration, and the long-term reuse of research products.

## Research repositories

Our collection of publication-related repositories is under development.

Each repository will provide its own documentation, citation information, and instructions for reproducing the associated scientific results.
